## Professional Overview

I am a **Software Engineer with 4+ years of experience** building production-ready applications across **mobile, web, backend, cloud, and AI-integrated ecosystems**.

My work focuses on building scalable, high-performance, user-friendly digital products using **Flutter, ReactJS, Python, Firebase, SQL, REST APIs, cloud services, analytics, and modern deployment workflows**.

I have delivered **25+ production apps and websites** across different domains, including food delivery, education, healthcare, devotional media, business platforms, service platforms, admin dashboards, automation systems, and AI-powered solutions.

I have collaborated with clients and distributed teams across **India, the US, Japan, Hong Kong, Nigeria, and more**, handling real product requirements from planning to production release.
